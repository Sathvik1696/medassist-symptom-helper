🛡️ Commitment to Patient Safety

Safety Comes First

MedAssist is designed to provide informational, educational guidance and does not replace professional medical diagnosis, clinical evaluation, or doctor prescribed treatment.

🚨

Emergency Detection Protocol

If your symptoms match acute clinical red flags (such as crushing chest pain, sudden numbness, or severe dyspnea), MedAssist halts standard informational processing immediately and displays urgent emergency instructions.

🩺

Non-Diagnostic Mandate

We describe potential concerns, common causes, and symptom patterns rather than offering definitive diagnosis. Always consult a qualified physician or healthcare provider for medical evaluation.
